# 💫 About Me:

I'm currently working on deepening my skills in deep learning and LLMs, building on projects like my serverless AQI Predictor (Random Forest, Hopsworks, GitHub Actions, Streamlit) from my Data Science internship at 10Pearls, and my full-stack Car Rental MERN app deployed on Vercel and Render. I'm looking to collaborate on AI/ML projects, especially anything involving generative AI, computer vision, or MLOps pipelines, and I'm always open to teaming up on hackathons or open-source work. I'm looking for help with scaling ML models into production and sharpening my understanding of MLOps best practices. I'm currently learning about deep learning architectures, LLMs, and the broader AI engineering stack (from classical ML to model deployment). Ask me about Python, the MERN stack, data science workflows, or anything related to building end-to-end ML pipelines. Fun fact: I enjoy exploring generative AI tools for image and video editing on the side, alongside following forex and commodity markets.
